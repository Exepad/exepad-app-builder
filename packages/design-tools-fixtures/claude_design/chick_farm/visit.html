03 / What to bring

Boots, ideally.

The pasture is real pasture, which is to say: muddy after rain, dewy in the morning, and, in spots, exactly as chickens left it. We have a few pairs of loaner boots in mismatched sizes by the gate.

  • ✓ Closed-toe shoes (boots best)
  • ✓ Cash or Venmo for the stand
  • ✓ Curiosity
  • ✗ Dogs · sorry, the hens get nervous
