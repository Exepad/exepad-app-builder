On the menu

Restaurants cooking with our eggs.

If you can't make it to the farm, the next best thing is eating breakfast at one of these places.

Hatchet & Whisk

Brunch spot doing a soft-scramble with our pastel mix. Worth the wait on Sundays.

Brattleboro · 11 mi

The Greenhorn

Farm-to-table tasting menu. Custard tart with our yolks, every fall.

Putney · 2.6 mi

Acorn & Ash

Wood-fired pizza. The "breakfast pie" is two of ours, cracked over Hilltop ricotta.

Brattleboro · 10 mi

Wren's Diner

Old-school counter diner, no relation to our daughter. Eggs any-which-way for $6.

Bellows Falls · 18 mi

Salt & Sage

Tasting menu in a tiny converted barn. Eight-course, includes our pickled eggs in spring.

Northampton · 43 mi

Buttercup & Co.

Breakfast bakery; their quiche has been on the menu since they opened in 2020.
