The personalities

A few we know especially well.

We don't name all 412. We name the ones that earn it — by being exceptionally kind, exceptionally weird, or exceptionally bossy.

Pepper · barred

Pepper

Plymouth Rock · est. 2014

Our very first hen. Twelve years old, mostly retired, still walks the porch most mornings. Lays maybe one egg a month, all of which we keep.

— hen #1.
Brunhilde · buff

Brunhilde

Buff Orpington · est. 2019

The undisputed queen of the main coop. If you stand still long enough she'll fly up and roost on your arm. Has bitten Joel exactly once, in 2021.

— matriarch.
Margot · ameraucana

Margot

Ameraucana · est. 2022

Lays the bluest egg in the flock — a real Tiffany blue. Refuses to lay anywhere except a specific cardboard box behind the feed bin.

— our color queen.
Doris · welsummer

Doris

Welsummer · est. 2020

Has escaped the run forty-three documented times. We added a second layer of fencing last year. She still gets out.

— freedom fighter.
Henrietta · marans

Henrietta

Marans · est. 2021

Stately, French, judgmental. Lays eggs so dark they look painted. Will tolerate being held for exactly seven seconds.

— our snob.
Biscuit · easter egger

Biscuit

Easter Egger · est. 2023

Wren's hen. Lays a soft pink egg, every other day. Comes when called, mostly.

— wren's girl.
