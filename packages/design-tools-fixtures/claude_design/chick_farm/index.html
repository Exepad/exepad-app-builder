01 / Real pasture

Grass under foot, sky overhead.

Our hens spend every daylight hour outside on rotating paddocks. We move them weekly so the land — and the bugs — keep up.

02 / Heritage breeds

Plymouth Rocks, Welsummers, Buff Orpingtons.

We raise the old breeds — slower-growing, prettier eggs, and built for actually living outside in a real Vermont winter.

Our story

We started with six hens and a borrowed coop.

That was 2014. Marta wanted better eggs than the grocery store could provide. Joel built a chicken tractor out of pallets. The first egg arrived on a Tuesday and we ate it together standing over the kitchen sink.

Twelve years later we're up to four hundred and change. We still know which hen lays which egg. We still eat the first one of each new pullet's life standing over the sink.
