# Test Prompts: Gamified Apps

Test prompts for evaluating the agent's ability to generate apps with gamification mechanics — scoring, achievements, leaderboards, progress tracking, quizzes with game elements, and interactive challenges. These exercise the intersection of app infrastructure (auth, CRUD, state) with game-like engagement patterns.

---

## Quiz & Trivia

1. "Create a trivia quiz app with 10 questions, scoring, a timer, and a results page showing pass/fail"
2. "Build a pub quiz app with multiple rounds, point tracking per round, and a final leaderboard"
3. "Make a knowledge assessment quiz for new employees with scoring, immediate feedback per question, and a certificate page for passing"
4. "Create a 'How well do you know your city?' quiz with multiple choice questions, fun facts after each answer, and a shareable score"
5. "Build a speed quiz game where each question has a 15-second countdown and bonus points for fast answers"
