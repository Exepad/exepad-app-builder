# Test Prompts: Animated Apps

Test prompts for evaluating the agent's ability to generate apps with rich animation — scroll-triggered entrances, hover/tap feedback, page transitions, staggered reveals, parallax effects, and motion personality matching. These prompts should exercise the platform's `Motion` namespace patterns inside `@exepad/sdk`.

---

## Scroll-Triggered & Entrance Animations

1. "Create a SaaS landing page where each section fades in as I scroll down"
2. "Build a portfolio website where project cards slide up and stagger in one by one as they enter the viewport"
3. "Make a nonprofit website where the impact stats count up with animation when they scroll into view"
4. "Create a product page where features reveal one at a time with a slide-up animation on scroll"
5. "Build a company website where the team member cards fade in with a staggered delay as you scroll to the team section"

## Hero & Page-Level Animation

6. "Create a landing page with a dramatic hero — headline types in letter by letter, subtitle fades in after, and the CTA button scales up last"
7. "Build a portfolio website centered on a full-screen home page hero with a parallax background image effect"
8. "Make a product launch page with a zoom-in hero animation and a scroll-triggered feature reveal below"
9. "Create a photography portfolio where the hero image slowly scales down as you scroll past it"
10. "Build a luxury brand landing page with slow, elegant fade transitions between sections"

## Hover & Interactive Feedback

11. "Create a pricing page where the plan cards lift up with a shadow on hover"
12. "Build a services page where each service card scales slightly and glows on hover"
13. "Make an agency website where all buttons have a satisfying press animation on click"
14. "Create a team page where hovering on a team member card reveals their bio with a smooth expand animation"
15. "Build a product grid where items tilt slightly on hover and snap back with a spring effect"

## Page Transitions

16. "Create a multi-page website with smooth slide-fade transitions between pages"
17. "Build a portfolio site where navigating between project pages uses a flip transition"
18. "Make a documentation site with fast, subtle fade transitions when switching pages"
19. "Create a storytelling website where each page slides up as you navigate through chapters"

## Stagger & Sequence Animations

20. "Build a features page where benefit cards appear one by one in a cascading waterfall"
21. "Create a testimonials page where review cards stagger in from the left"
22. "Make a gallery page where images load in with a staggered grid reveal pattern"
23. "Create a pricing comparison page where the columns animate in from left to right with a 100ms delay between each"
24. "Build a FAQ page where accordion items slide in one after another on page load"

## Motion Personality — Professional / Corporate

25. "Create a professional consulting firm website with subtle, understated animations — no flashy effects, just smooth fades and clean transitions"
26. "Build a corporate B2B dashboard where transitions are fast and functional, not decorative"
27. "Make a law firm website — animations should be elegant and minimal, nothing playful"

## Motion Personality — Playful / Creative

28. "Create a fun, bouncy landing page for a kids' coding school — everything should feel alive and playful"
29. "Build a game studio website with energetic animations — bouncing elements, springy cards, and bold transitions"
30. "Make a creative agency portfolio where every element has personality — hover tilts, springy reveals, staggered entrances"
31. "Create a birthday party invitation website that's vibrant and animated — confetti effects, bouncing text, playful transitions"

## Motion Personality — Minimal / Elegant

32. "Build a minimalist architecture studio website with very slow, refined fade-in animations"
33. "Create an art gallery website where images reveal with a gentle scale-up and everything breathes"
34. "Make a luxury watch brand landing page with cinematic, slow-motion scroll effects"

## Animation on Forms & Data Apps

35. "Create a multi-step form where each step slides in from the right with a smooth transition"
36. "Build a dashboard where stat cards animate their values counting up on load"
37. "Make a survey form (Typeform-style) where each question fades in with a subtle slide-up"
38. "Create an admin panel where modals scale in when opening and fade out when closing"
39. "Build a CRM where adding a new contact row animates it into the table with a layout animation"

## Conditional & State-Driven Animation

40. "Create a landing page where a success banner shakes to grab attention when a form is submitted"
41. "Build a todo app where completed items animate out with a fade and strikethrough"
42. "Make a notification system where toast messages slide in from the top and auto-dismiss with a slide-out"
43. "Create a dashboard where chart cards pulse briefly when their data updates"

## Parallax & Scroll-Linked

44. "Build a storytelling website with a long-scrolling home page and parallax backgrounds that move at different speeds per section"
45. "Create a product showcase where the product image rotates slightly as you scroll"
46. "Make a timeline page where events slide in alternately from left and right as you scroll down"
47. "Build a landing page with a scroll progress bar at the top that fills as you scroll through the page"

## Stress Tests / Edge Cases

48. "Create a website with absolutely no animations — everything static, no transitions, no hover effects"
49. "Build a website that respects reduced-motion preferences — full animations by default but completely static for users who prefer reduced motion"
50. "Make a landing page with very heavy animation — every section animates, cards stagger, hero has parallax, buttons bounce — test that it still performs well"
