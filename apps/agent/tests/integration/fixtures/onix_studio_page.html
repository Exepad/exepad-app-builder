04 / Approach

Five things we will not stop saying.

signed by all partners
updated 2026.03
01 —

We don't ship demos. We ship systems with on-call rotations.

If nobody is paged when it breaks, it isn't in production. Every engagement ships with a runbook, an SLO, and a name on the rotation — usually one of ours, for at least 90 days.

02 —

The eval harness is the product. The model is just the implementation.

We write the test set before we write the agent. If you can't measure it, we won't build it. If your team can't run the evals without us, we haven't finished.

03 —

Most of the work is not the model.

It's schema design, retrieval that earns its keep, queue topology, refusal policy, and the fifteen tedious integrations between your CRM and the thing your CEO saw on stage. We do that part on purpose.

04 —

If the answer is a chatbot, we'll tell you.

Sometimes it's a worker, a webhook, an internal tool, or a much smaller model in a much quieter place. We pick the boring solution when the boring solution wins, which is most of the time.

05 —

We leave less behind than the people before us.

Two repos. One pipeline. A test suite your team owns. Documentation a new hire can read on a Tuesday. The opposite of a 60-page Notion site that nobody opens after the kickoff.
